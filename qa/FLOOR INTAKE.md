# Floor survey handoff

Copy the block into the team’s survey notes. Fill it on the event floor with a second walker. Use the [course capture sheet](physical-walk.md) for the full walk and blind walk. This form is blank and provides no route evidence.

```text
FLOOR SURVEY | Course revision: ___ | Date and time (Dubai): ___
Observer: ___ | Second walker: ___ | Evidence log reference: ___
Allowed area, access limits and conditions: ___
Start state ID, exact stop cue and observed facing: ___

TARGET (repeat for each guide target)
Guide ID: ___ | Exact sign or distinctive cue: ___
Approach/stop state IDs: ___ | Access and endpoint proof, or reason excluded: ___

STATE (repeat for starts, turns, junctions, switch points and endpoints)
Survey state ID: ___ | Physical stop point and exact cue: ___
Observed facing: ___ | Cue first visible from: ___ | Safe to stop? ___
Similar signs, movable cues or visibility issues: ___
Checked by / Dubai date and time / specific evidence reference: ___

DIRECTED LEG (repeat for each direction walked)
Leg ID and from → to state IDs: ___ | Exact English instruction heard: ___
Entry pose and observed exit pose: ___ | Stop cue and facing acknowledgment: ___
Conditions and walk duration: ___ | Second walker result: ___
Checked by / Dubai date and time / specific evidence reference: ___

COVERAGE (repeat for each checked start or switch state and destination)
State ID → guide ID: ___ | Ordered leg IDs and endpoint stop proof: ___
Verified by, or gap and reason: ___
If the switch was between states: actual observation and separately walked recovery,
or HOLD with reason: ___
```

Guide IDs to account for: `meeting_room`, `conference_room`, `call_booth_1`, `call_booth_2`, `call_booth_3`, `call_booth_4`, `team_tables`, `kitchen`. Confirm which observed booth maps to each ID. List distinct team table targets separately. Mark unwalked, obstructed or ambiguous legs unusable. Check reverse legs separately.

For review against the [route contract](../app/lib/route-contract.md), keep separate survey state IDs for different headings at one stop. Group those states by physical stop and exact cue when handing them to the route owner. The planner uses an anchor plus a pose. Flag repeated cues at different stops or instructions the planner cannot express. Record a checked compass heading or the exact cue faced, and flag any leg without a single checked exit pose. Hand over the evidence and gaps for review before any map entry.
