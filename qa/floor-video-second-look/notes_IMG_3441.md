## E. Confusing or risky spots for a robot operator or a voice agent

- **Lots of glass.** Revolving door, side glass doors, the full curved glass wall, glass double doors. Glass is hard for robot cameras and distance sensors. At night the glass is full of reflections (t=0 to 1.5 and t=20 to 21 are almost unreadable). SEEN.
- **Very shiny floors.** Both the cream and the grey floors mirror the ceiling lights and the blue LED glow. A robot may see "holes" or fake objects. SEEN.
- **Floor changes** from cream to grey along a curve (no step seen). Also dark door mats at the three entrance doors. Mats can catch robot wheels or feet (INFERRED).
- **Look-alike things.** Many plants in steel pots (at least 4), several identical dark green benches along the glass wall, three entrance doors side by side, two navy seats (the wavy bench and the sofa at the far right), grey walls that all look the same. The voice agent should not say "the plant" or "the bench" alone.
- **Silver column and steel pots** are reflective, so the robot camera may get odd readings (INFERRED).
- **Low and odd-shaped furniture.** The navy wavy bench is low and curved with thin legs. Hard to see from a robot's height (INFERRED).
- **Reception desk corner** sticks out (t=14 to 17), with a tall front panel. The walker passes close to it.
- **A person near the door** (t=12 to 19) and a revolving door that may turn. Moving things near the entrance. SEEN.
- **A fire alarm, CCTV dome and exit signs** are small landmarks but they are the same on every wall, so they are poor landmarks.
- **Dark outside.** The video is at night. Daytime light may change how the glass and floor look (INFERRED).
- **Very few strong landmarks in the grey lobby.** The best ones are the yellow signs, the navy wavy bench and the glass double doors.
