## E. Confusing or risky spots for an operator or a voice agent

1. **Look-alike signs (SEEN).** The green speech bubble with a black arrow appears on the **purple pillar**, probably on the **orange pillar** (green blob, UNSURE), and on the **maroon wall near the yellow doors in the marble hall**. An agent that says "walk to the green speech bubble" could mean any of them. Always say the pillar colour.
2. **Look-alike pillars (SEEN).** Purple, yellow, blue and orange pillars in a row on the right of the walkway. They all have a board or a bubble. A "yellow" thing also appears as the yellow-framed doors in the marble hall and the yellow wooden door frames in the glass corridor.
3. **The striped walkway (SEEN).** Shiny white floor with dark stripes across it. It reflects lights, which can confuse cameras and floor sensors. It looks the same along its whole length, so the operator cannot tell how far they are without the pillars.
