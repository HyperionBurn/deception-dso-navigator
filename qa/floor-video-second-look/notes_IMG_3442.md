## E. Confusing or risky spots

1. **Look-alike doors (SEEN).** Many doors are the same: grey steel door, steel frame, riveted steel panel above. This is true for the lounge doors (toilets, 014, 015) and for the toilet doors beside the blue sofas. A voice agent saying "the grey door with the steel frame" would be wrong most of the time. Use text on the door (number, "TOILET") instead.
2. **Two sets of toilets and two sets of glass double doors with the same X pattern (SEEN).** One pair of doors at the start of the corridor, one pair at the end. They look the same. The corridor start has lifts and stairs next to it; the end opens into a cream-floored glass hall.
3. **Two yellow "BLOCK" signs (SEEN).** One at the start ("THIS WAY ← A BLOCK") and one at t=66 (only "BLOCK" readable). They look alike.
4. **Shiny floors (SEEN).** The corridor floor is mirror-like with blue LED reflections that look like markings. The cream terrazzo floor is also very shiny. A robot's floor sensors or an operator's eyes may confuse reflections with objects or with step edges.
5. **Glass and mirror surfaces (SEEN).** Glass doors (some clear, some frosted), glass walls on both sides of the hall, stainless-steel lift doors and steel panels (mirror-like), tilted blue glass panels, a glass railing next to the machine. Robot depth cameras may not see clear glass. Reflections of people and plants appear in the glass.
6. **Doors that were open in this video (SEEN).** Both glass double doors (start and end of the corridor) were propped open. They have push handles, so they may be closed at other times. I cannot tell if they are always open.
7. **Narrow places (INFERRED).** The corridor looks about 2.5 to 3 m wide. The glass hall has plants in tall steel pots on both sides and looks about 2.5 to 3 m wide. Only one person at a time can pass with a robot. Not measured.
8. **People (SEEN).** A man filming at the start, a man crossing at t=4, a man walking toward the camera in the corridor (t=48-52), a person sitting in G-013. Doors can open into the corridor.
9. **Low and odd-shaped furniture (SEEN).** Low wavy blue bench at the start. Pod sofa on a carpet in the lounge. The blue wavy sofa and the wing chairs have curved legs and overhangs that are hard to see from a low camera. The green machine has small wheels.
10. **Tilted glass panels in the last lobby (SEEN).** They lean. Their bottom edge sticks out into the floor and is hard to see. They stand in front of the walls near the sofas.
11. **Stairs or a drop (INFERRED, UNSURE).** At t=66-70 there is a glass railing with a steel handrail on the left of the green machine. It looks like it goes along stairs. I could not see the stairs themselves. Keep the robot well to the right of the machine until someone checks.
12. **Stair door and lifts at the start (SEEN).** Right next to the corridor entrance. A robot should not be sent through the stair door (push-bar fire door).
13. **Very low floor mats (SEEN).** The cream mat in the corridor (t=15-22) and the cream carpet under the pod sofa look flat. A robot should be fine, but check the edges.
14. **Camera shake and blur (SEEN).** t=7-10, t=14, t=21, t=38-39, t=45-47, t=53-57 are blurred. Details in those seconds are less reliable.
15. **Lighting colour (SEEN).** The corridor is lit strongly blue. Walls look purple or lilac in some frames. The cream hall looks yellow-green. The white balance changes a lot, so colour words like "purple" or "yellow" depend on the lighting.
