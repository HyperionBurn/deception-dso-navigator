## 3. Where the helpers and the repo agree
The spine our map uses is the same in both: start at the pitch room end, walk straight toward reception, purple pillar then yellow pillar on the right, cafeteria on the left, left turn about 90 degrees at the yellow pillar. The repo found nothing about call booths, meeting room or conference room, and neither did the helpers (names). 3441 and 3442 are outside the area in both.

## 4. What is new or different (check these on a walk)
1. **Possible call booths (INFERRED, I saw the frames).** Three glass doors with wooden frames and a phone-shaped frosted pattern in the marble hall (3440 about 99-103 s and 123 s, frame `3440_h0200`). The repo says no call booth appears in any frame. These could be them. No text on the doors says so. The challenge has four call booths, so look for a fourth.
2. **"Pizza counter" may not be the menu boards.** A "PIZZA" sign is behind the lattice screen next to the walkway (32 s, `3440_h0064`; the repo's text reader found it too). The two menu boards are deeper inside the cafeteria (42 s and 60 s). Our map gives the kitchen anchor the alias "pizza counter". They may be two different spots. UNVERIFIED.
3. **The cafeteria is not at the corner.** The entrance is a gap in the navy plant boxes, easy to miss (INFERRED). The menu boards are across the seating area (distance unknown). "Turn left, walk straight" may not be enough words for an operator. The helper also could not say whether the gap is level with the yellow pillar or earlier (it sits "after the lattice screen", which faces the purple pillar). Settle this by walking.
4. **The pitch room "door" is a gap.** There is no door between the start room and the walkway, only an open space beside the black wall and the blue pillar. A plain white door inside the room has no sign. Telling the operator to find "the pitch room door" may confuse them. "The black wall with the steel bin" is in our aliases and is easier to see.
5. **Look-alike signs.** The same green speech bubble with a black arrow is on the purple pillar, on a maroon wall near the yellow-framed doors in the marble hall, and probably on the orange pillar. Always say the colour.
6. **Menu board words are UNVERIFIED.** The helper read "CAFETERIA", "SMOOTHIES / SOFT DRINKS" and "PANINI / PASTA" on the boards and shutters. At full size I cannot read the board text, and the repo's text reader found none. Do not put those words in the map until someone reads them on site.
7. **Robot-blocking spots:** narrow steel turnstiles, shiny striped floor and glass everywhere (reflections), people at the tables, cables on the cafeteria floor, a small robot kneeling in the start room.
