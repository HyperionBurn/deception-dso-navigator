01 YOUR THREE ROUTES

   You film three routes on the robot, with the MangoBot operator walking it. At least two
   languages in every route, ideally with Arabic, and the agent translates for the operator.

1 The easy route                                          YOU CHOOSE

   Pick the request and the destination. Show the whole flow working once: request, route, stop,

   confirmation.

2  Background music                                       WE SUPPLY THE MUSIC

   We play a regular music track while the visitor talks. You cannot choose or change it. Your
   agent still has to understand the visitor and get the robot there.

3  Change of destination                                  WE SET THE CHANGE

   Halfway along the route, a second person asks the robot to go somewhere else. We tell you the
   new destination when you record, so it cannot be built in beforehand.
